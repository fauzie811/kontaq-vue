# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Muslim learners and Quran study community members tracking daily Quran reading, tadabbur, quizzes, materials, evaluations, and infaq.

## Product Purpose

Kontaq provides a cohesive web platform for Quran study, reflection (tadabbur), interactive quizzes, learning materials, community discussion forums, performance reports, certificates, and infaq contributions.

## Positioning

An integrated, community-centered Quranic learning and reflection platform combining daily reading, structured learning materials, interactive evaluations, and social forum interactions into one seamless web experience.

## Operating Context

Used by community members on desktop and mobile web browsers during daily study sessions, group learning activities, personal reflections, and community events.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack**: Vue 3, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4, Vue Router, Radix Vue, Lucide Icons
- **Key Modules**: Authentication (Login, Register, Password Reset), Quran Reading & View (`/quran/:chapter`), Tadabbur (`/tadabbur`), Learning Materials (`/materials`), Quizzes (`/quizzes`), Evaluations (`/evaluations`), Community Forum (`/forum`), Infaq (`/infaq`), Reports & Certificates (`/reports`, `/certificates`), Announcements & User Profile.
- **Brand Colors**: `#40835c` (Forest Green - Primary) and `#e79b44` (Warm Amber - Accent).

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: Kontaq
- **Primary Brand Color**: `#40835c` (Forest Green) — used for main navigation, primary actions, and core branding.
- **Accent Brand Color**: `#e79b44` (Warm Amber) — used for highlights, badges, progress indicators, and secondary call-to-actions.

## Evidence on Hand

- Runnable Vue 3 + Tailwind CSS codebase with defined router paths and components in `src/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Clarity & Focus**: Minimal distractions to support deep reflection (tadabbur) and study.
2. **Community & Connection**: Foster continuous engagement through forums, announcements, and progress sharing.
3. **Respectful & Harmonic Identity**: Use serene Forest Green and warm Amber tones to evoke reflection, warmth, and trust.
